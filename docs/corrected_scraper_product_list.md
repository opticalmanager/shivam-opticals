# Shivam Optical — Corrected Canonical Product Catalog for Scrapers (138 Products Audit)

## Executive Summary

Out of the **138 inactive products** extracted from the original physical inventory stock sheets (stored in `D:\Downloads\product list`), an exhaustive line-by-line verification against the ledger scans and global optical databases reveals:

- **79 Commercial / Designer Eyewear Products**: Verified authentic models from international optical manufacturers (Safilo, Luxottica, Menrad, Marcolin, Sterling Meta Plast). Their names have been systematically corrected to their true canonical model codes, fixing OCR misreads, dropped prefix letters (`FC`, `TH`, `BE`, `SC`, `AX`, `CA`), transposed digits, and misclassified brands.
- **50 Private Atelier / House Collection Products**: Exclusively physical, in-house clinic and wholesale inventory (Daniel Morris, Frank Lux, Zoe Millar, Shivam Atelier, Eyeplayer, Insist, Paradox, Prodigy, Qloo, etc.). These do **not** exist in online commercial retail catalogs and must be **skipped** by web scrapers.
- **2 Unbranded Wholesale Lots**: Generic sunglasses lots (`Scott METAL` and `Scott SHEET`) lacking specific manufacturer model codes.
- **7 Phantom OCR Artifacts & Duplicates**: Synthetic scraps generated by flawed OCR reading across column boundaries (dates, price numbers) or duplicate scans (`so-carrera-*` and `carrera-ca-prw-4-in`).
